Challenger and Futures Matches of no interest as they are prone to match fixing, occasional lack of interest in winning, tanking, and irregular form due to the nature of this level of tour. The only time a Challenger will need to appear in the JBet system is whenever they attain an ATP match. In that case (ATP versus Challenger Player) their statistics and results will be present in the JBet Database. For the other case (Challenger versus Challenger versus Futures), the Match Results and Statistics are practically meaningless and therefore not represented purposefully.
